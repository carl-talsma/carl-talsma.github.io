Welcome to the Electionizer Application!

Electionizer is a pet project of mine to predict election outcomes based on a variety of data. Electionizer uses machine learning algorithms to analyze and forecast election results using polling data, demographic information, historical voting patterns, and Google search trends for candidates. I'll be updating polls periodically and posting updates on model performance, predictions, and insights in the lead up to the 2026 midterm elections. Check out any updates below and feel free to reach out if you have any questions or suggestions for the project!
